The in-game mod manager (F7): browse mods from the sources you watch, install, update and disable them, and see what other players have.

What it does
- My mods lists what you have, updates first. Browse shows everything your sources offer, grouped by source.
- Players shows who has which mods, and the mods other players have that you do not.
- Sources: the main repos are built in, community sources come from pull requests to the manager's repo, and you can add your own.
- Mod settings: change any loaded mod's settings in the game (switches, sliders, numbers, keys and colours), with a reset button for each.
- Mods reload in-game with no restart; ones that cannot say so.
